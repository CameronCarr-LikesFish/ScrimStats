What the numbers mean

All stats count words and phrases from the transcripts, so they can't hear tone or sarcasm and inherit any transcription mistakes. They're best for trends over weeks and months, not judging a single game. Phrase lists live in Settings\Callout types.txt; who a sentence is about is judged from words like I / you / he (我 / 你 / 他 in Chinese). Chinese lines are sorted using Chinese phrases on the original wording, plus the English translation.

Parts of the game. Early (laning) lasts until the first outer tower in that player's lane falls, either team's, and at most until 20:00. Junglers: the first outer tower anywhere. The lane comes from the player's role on the roster (no role = bot lane). Mid game runs until 30:00, or until the first inhibitor falls if that's sooner. Late game is the rest.
