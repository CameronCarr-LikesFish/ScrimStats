LoL Scrim Comms: how to use
===========================

Double-click "LoL Scrim Comms.exe". One window, three steps.


1. RECORD GAMES  (before your scrim block)

   Click "Start recording" and leave the app open. It waits for a game,
   records each one on its own, and keeps going between games. It only
   records the game on THIS PC, so run it on the PC you play from.

   You can test it any time with Practice Tool.


2. TRANSCRIBE COMMS  (after your scrim block)

   In Discord: /stop Craig, open the link, download Multi-track -> FLAC.
   (Craig deletes recordings after 7 days, so do this soon.)

   Click "Add Craig download..." and pick the .zip (or put the .zip in the
   "Craig downloads" folder yourself). Then click "Transcribe".

   It takes roughly 30-60 minutes for a 3-hour block (longer if there is a
   lot of Chinese, which also gets translated). You can stop it any
   time; finished speakers are kept and it picks up where it left off.
   It runs entirely on this PC. Nobody's voice is uploaded.

   CHINESE: English and Mandarin are both understood, even mixed in one
   sentence ("Kai'Sa 没有 flash 了"). Chinese lines are kept in Chinese,
   marked (中文) in the transcript, and followed by a rough English
   translation. The stats read the Chinese directly, using the Chinese
   phrases in Settings\Callout types.txt, so they don't depend on the
   translation being perfect. If the translation keeps getting a slang term
   wrong, add a fix to Settings\Corrections.txt  (e.g.  my pot => my bad).


3. REVIEW

   "Edit roster": one line per player:  Name | Discord name | Riot ID
   Do this once, and again whenever the team changes.

   "Open dashboard": builds the stats and opens them in your browser.
   Every stat is explained at the bottom of the dashboard.


FOLDERS
   Craig downloads    put Craig .zip files here
   Game recordings    what the game recorder saved (one file per game)
   Transcripts        who said what, when (.txt files are easy to read)
   Settings           the word lists and roster (open with Notepad)
   _data, _internal   the app's own files; no need to touch these

   Dashboard.html and Comms stats.csv appear here after "Open dashboard".
   The CSV opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

You can move this whole folder anywhere (Desktop, Documents...). Keep
everything inside it together.


SETTINGS FILES  (Settings folder, open with Notepad)
   Roster.txt              who's who
   Callout types.txt       which phrases count as shotcalling, enemy info,
                           my status, item timers, timers, and so on
   League words.txt        words the speech model listens for; most
                           important first
   Champion nicknames.txt  J4, Asol, MF...
   Corrections.txt         fixes for words it keeps mishearing,
                           e.g.  zayen => Zaahen
