ScrimStats: how to use
======================

Double-click "ScrimStats.exe". Everything happens in that one window.
The sections on the left:

HOME
   1. Record games: click "Start recording" before your scrim block and
      leave the app open. It records every game on its own, as a player
      or a spectator.
   2. Transcribe comms: after scrims, /stop Craig in Discord and download
      Multi-track -> FLAC. Click "Add Craig download...", then "Transcribe".
      A 3-hour block takes about 10-15 minutes on the graphics card.
      Nothing is uploaded.
   The Activity box shows what's happening.

DASHBOARD
   Each player's comms stats, and how they change over time. Pick a roster
   (Varsity, JV...) and a part of the game (early / mid / late) at the top.
   Further down: champion win rates, every flame and negative line word for
   word (click "More context" to see what led up to it, and mark each one
   Flame, Negative or Neither), and what was said between games.
   Every stat is explained at the bottom.

TRANSCRIPTS
   Read and search who said what. Chinese lines show a rough English
   translation underneath.

ROSTERS  (do this once, and when the team changes)
   One tab per roster (Varsity, JV...): "Add roster" makes another. Add
   your players and set each one's role. Then, in the lists of names found
   in your recordings, choose who each Discord name and in-game account
   belongs to. Save. A sub on two rosters: add them to both with the same
   name.

GAMES
   Every recorded game. If you spectated a game with a delay (the usual
   spectator delay is 3 minutes), set it here so the game lines up with
   the comms.
   Spectating? League doesn't tell spectators about dragons, barons or
   heralds. Have one of the five players run ScrimStats too and click
   "Start recording". After scrims they send you the new files from their
   "Game recordings" folder, and you click "Add a player's recordings...".

SETTINGS
   The word lists the app uses (callout types, League words, champion
   nicknames, corrections), and buttons to open the app's folders.

You can move this whole folder anywhere (Desktop, Documents...). Keep
everything inside it together.

FOLDERS (you don't need to touch these)
   Craig downloads, Game recordings, Transcripts, Settings: the app's data.
   _data, _internal: the app's own files.
   Dashboard.html and Comms stats.csv: copies of the stats. The CSV opens
   in Excel or Google Sheets.
